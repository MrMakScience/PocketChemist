    Drawing help and shortcuts
    • Click empty space to place a carbon, then click that atom to add a bonded carbon in a zigzag.
    • Drag from an atom to choose the direction (30° steps). Drop on another atom to join them.
    • Click a bond to cycle single, double, triple.
    • Pick N, O, F, Cl, Br or I in the toolbar, then click an atom to change it. Every corner and end is a carbon unless labelled.
    • Ring buttons place a ring; click an atom first to attach the ring to it.
    • With the pointer over an atom: C N O F L (Cl) R (Br) I. Over a bond: 1 2 3. Delete removes it.
    • Keyboard only: Tab to the canvas, press Enter to place a carbon, ← → to move between atoms, Enter to grow a bond, then the same letter and number keys.
    What this tool covers
    • Up to 20 carbons; parent chain or ring up to 10 carbons.
    • Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, single carbocycles, and branches of any complexity.
    • Halogens, alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, acyl halides, amines (primary, secondary, tertiary), amides and nitriles.
    • Parent choice follows the classic school rules: most main groups, then the ring or chain with more carbons (a ring wins a tie), then most C=C / C≡C, then the longest chain, then most substituents, then lowest locants.
    • Not covered yet: benzene rings, fused or bridged rings, N-heterocycles, imines, nitro compounds, anhydrides, lactones and stereochemistry (E/Z, R/S).
