Avogadro’s law: volume tells you moles

At the same temperature and pressure, equal volumes of any gases hold the same number of moles.

pV = nRT same p, T and V → same n

So gas volume is proportional to moles. You never need to convert back to moles. Read the volume ratio straight from the coefficients in the equation.

Use one unit all the way through (1 dm³ = 1000 cm³). Relative atomic masses and molar volume are given only in the questions that need them.

From gas volumes to formulae

A volume ratio is a mole ratio, and a mole ratio is the ratio of coefficients. Atoms are never lost, so counting atoms on each side gives the unknown formula.
