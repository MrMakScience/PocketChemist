VSEPR Quick Facts
  • Lone pairs exert more repulsive force than bonding pairs.
  • Repulsion order: Lone Pair-Lone Pair > Lone Pair-Bonding Pair > Bonding Pair-Bonding Pair.
  • Lone pairs demand larger spatial angles, pushing bonds closer together.
Stability Test

Grab any atom (or lone pair lobe) and drag it away from its position. Electron pairs repel each other, so as soon as you let go they push one another back and wobble into the same arrangement as before, because that arrangement has the minimum repulsion.
