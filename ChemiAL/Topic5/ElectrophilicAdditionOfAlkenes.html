    Key words

    Electrophile
    Electron-pair acceptor. Here it is the δ+ end of the reagent.
    Nucleophile
    Electron-pair donor: a lone pair that attacks a δ+ or + carbon.
    Curved arrow
    Shows a pair of electrons moving, from the tail to the head.
    Intermediate
    A short-lived species formed between the reactants and the product.
    Markovnikov's rule
    In HX addition, X goes to the carbon that has more alkyl groups.
